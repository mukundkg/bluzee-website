PRIVACY-POLICY
BLUZEE / INFORMATION
Privacy policy
Information about this website and its services.
Website enquiries
We collect the information you provide to contact Bluzee: your name, email address, selected offering, message, optional organisation and acknowledgement of the enquiry notice. We use this information to review and respond to your request.
Formspree processes and stores website form submissions and sends notifications to Bluzee’s Namecheap-hosted email. The website and its media are hosted through Cloudflare. Formspree may process request information such as IP address and browser details for service operation and security, including in the United States and other countries where it operates.
Records and choices
Enquiry records can exist in both Formspree and email correspondence. Formspree’s available submission history depends on the account plan and does not automatically delete email copies. You can contact Bluzee about retention, access, correction or deletion of your enquiry details.
The form asks for permission to process your enquiry; it does not include a mailing-list subscription. Please avoid passwords, payment details, identity documents and confidential business information in this initial message.
Website preferences and external services
Your motion preference can be saved in your browser. Links to WhatsApp, social platforms and external registration forms open services governed by their own privacy notices. Review those notices before sharing information.
Service and product data
Data handling for a customer project or product deployment depends on the relevant agreement, hosting arrangement, access controls and configuration. This website notice does not replace those terms.
